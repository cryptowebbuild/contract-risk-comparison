## The three clauses that mattered

### 13. Limitation of Liability  `5 -> 2`

**Risk theme:** Unlimited liability

Nothing in this Agreement limits <del>either party’s</del> liability for <del>breach of confidentiality, breach of intellectual property rights,</del> <ins>death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud,</ins> or any liability that cannot be limited by law.

Subject to the above, each party’s <ins>total aggregate</ins> liability <del>is unlimited.</del> <ins>arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed 100% of the Fees paid or payable under this Agreement.</ins>

<ins>Neither party shall be liable for loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of business, loss of anticipated savings, loss of data, or any indirect or consequential loss.</ins>

**Why it matters:** This is the clause that decides whether one bad project can end the business. Under v1 the Provider’s maximum exposure is unbounded while its maximum fee is BDT 12,00,000; the cap in v2 is exactly that fee. Quantified exposure moves from unlimited to BDT 12,00,000.

**Fallback position:** Accept 100% of fees plus a separate, higher cap for data-protection incidents if required by the Client’s group policy.

### 7. Intellectual Property  `5 -> 2`

**Risk theme:** IP ownership of reusable components

<del>All</del> <ins>Upon receipt of all Fees, the Provider assigns to the Client all</ins> intellectual property rights in the Deliverables, <del>including all pre-existing tools, frameworks, components and know-how incorporated in them, shall vest absolutely in the Client upon payment of all Fees.</del> <ins>excluding Pre-Existing Materials.</ins>

The Provider <del>hereby assigns to</del> <ins>grants</ins> the Client <del>all such rights with full title guarantee.</del> <ins>a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, modify and sublicense the Pre-Existing Materials solely as incorporated in the Deliverables.</ins>

<ins>The Provider retains ownership of Pre-Existing Materials and may use, licence and reuse them without restriction, including for other clients.</ins>

<ins>The Provider may display the Deliverables in its portfolio and on its website unless the Client objects in writing within 30 (thirty) days of delivery.</ins>

**Why it matters:** As drafted, v1 transfers ownership of the Provider’s entire component library — the asset that is licensed to every other client — for a single BDT 12,00,000 project, and grants “full title guarantee” which the Provider cannot give over third-party elements. This is the highest-value single line in the negotiation.

**Fallback position:** If the Client insists on owning components bespoke to them, agree a list in Schedule A of named bespoke components; keep the library out.

### 12. Indemnity  `5 -> 3`

**Risk theme:** Mutual, uncapped indemnity

<del>Each party</del> <ins>The Provider</ins> shall indemnify the <del>other</del> <ins>Client</ins> against <del>all losses, claims, damages, costs and expenses arising from any breach</del> <ins>direct losses finally awarded by a court or tribunal in respect</ins> of <del>this Agreement or any negligent act or omission, including all claims</del> <ins>a third-party claim</ins> that the <del>Deliverables</del> <ins>Deliverables, as delivered and unmodified,</ins> infringe <del>the</del> <ins>an</ins> intellectual property <del>rights of any third party.</del> <ins>right in Bangladesh.</ins>

The indemnity <del>in this Clause</del> is <del>not</del> subject to <del>any limitation of liability.</del> <ins>the Client notifying the Provider promptly, allowing the Provider to control the defence, and not settling the claim without consent.</ins>

<ins>The indemnity does not apply where the claim arises from Client Materials, Client instructions, Third-Party Materials, or modifications made by anyone other than the Provider.</ins>

<ins>The Provider’s total liability under this Clause is subject to Clause 13.</ins>

**Why it matters:** v1 exposes the Provider to the Client’s business losses on any breach, with no cap. The v2 version keeps the market-standard IP indemnity the Client actually needs, while removing indemnity cover for the Client’s own losses.

**Fallback position:** Accept a mutual IP indemnity; do not accept an indemnity for consequential loss.
