# Contract risk comparison

A clause-by-clause comparison of a 22-clause services agreement - client standard form (v1) against the supplier's counter-draft (v2) - produced with AutoClaw's Legal Assistant.

**Synthetic file.** Both parties, the addresses and the contract value are fictional. No client, personal or financial data appears in any document here. Not legal advice, not for execution.

## What is in this repo

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | the landing page - headline numbers, Clause 13 redlined inline, all 22 clauses scored, verification table |
| `REDLINE.md` | the clause-by-clause redline in Markdown: all 22 clauses in a table, plus full markup for the three clauses that mattered |

The Word files themselves (v1, v2, the 202-revision tracked redline, the 5-sheet workbook and the 4-page memo) are attached to the original X post; images of each are in that thread.

## Headline numbers

- 22 clauses reviewed, **19 changed**
- **202 tracked changes** in the Word redline
- **34 risk points** removed, scoring each clause 1-5 for the supplier's exposure before and after
- Three clauses sat at severity 5 in v1: **7 (IP), 12 (indemnity), 13 (liability)**
- Maximum liability: **unlimited -> 100% of fees (BDT 12,00,000)**
- Cash received before build: **20% -> 70%** (BDT 2,40,000 -> 8,40,000)
- 8 statutory checkpoints, **4 expressly flagged "to verify"** rather than presented as checked

## Method

1. Both versions compared clause by clause; every change described and classified.
2. Each clause scored 1-5 for the supplier's exposure in each version, with a written fallback position.
3. Legal propositions traced to statutory provisions, with an explicit verification status for each.
4. Results assembled into a memo addressed to the client, not to a file.

The reusable output is the scoring column, not the redline: it turns a list of edits into a negotiation order - three must-hold clauses, seven to protect margin, four tradeable, four give-aways.

## Viewing the real redline

Open the `.docx` from the X post and select **Review -> Display for Review -> All Markup**. Deleted text appears struck through in red; insertions appear underlined in blue.
